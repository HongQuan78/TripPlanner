Tràng An Scenic Landscape Complex

◎ Ninh Bình, Vietnam

A UNESCO World Heritage mixed site, Tràng An threads a maze of jade-green waterways between towering limestone karsts and hidden grottoes. Wooden sampan boats, rowed by local guides, drift through low cave tunnels to reach ancient temples and forgotten valleys. It is one of the most striking landscapes in northern Vietnam — quiet, cinematic, and best explored slowly by water.

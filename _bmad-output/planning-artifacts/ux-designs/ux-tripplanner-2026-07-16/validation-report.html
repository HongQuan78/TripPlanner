tripplanner — UX Design Validation Report

Attraction Detail (Azure) · DESIGN.md + EXPERIENCE.md spine pair

This is a tight, downstream-ready spine pair: every {path.to.token} reference resolves, component names line up across both files, the DESIGN section order is canonical, and all eight EXPERIENCE defaults are present with genuinely load-bearing invented sections (Map / Open-Now / Nearby / Add-to-Trip / Data handling). A consumer can source-extract it cleanly and build. Flow, component, bloat, and inheritance discipline are all strong; token, state, visual-reference, and shape-fit coverage are adequate with fixable gaps. The one rubric finding with real teeth is contrast: the palette is asserted "AA verified" but two load-bearing muted grays (label-muted, na) fall below AA on white, and downstream code mirrors tokens verbatim. Two required-when-applicable sections triggered by the memlog (Responsive, Inspiration & Anti-patterns) are absent — the breakpoint info exists but is scattered, and DESIGN even points to a "behavioral breakpoint contract" section that EXPERIENCE never formalizes.

The accessibility lens sharpens that one crack into the report's dominant story. Where the rubric flagged two failing grays, the adversarial contrast audit measures five load-bearing text combinations below WCAG 2.1 AA — the na gray (2.45:1) and the amber star rating (2.02:1) fail even the 3:1 large/graphic threshold, and the teal taxonomy text, the open-now status badge, and the info-row keys all fail normal-text AA at 12px or smaller. The frontmatter's "Azure palette verified for AA text on its intended surfaces" is false as measured. Beyond contrast, the hero's legibility guarantee is asserted rather than engineered (weak-scrim zone under the eyebrow and title), the logged-out Add-to-Trip uses a disabled pattern that removes the whole conversion path from the tab order, and no focus-visible token exists to verify against 1.4.11. The spine is structurally sound but should be treated as build-blocked on contrast and the hero scrim until those combinations are darkened and the scrim floor is specified.

2. Token completeness

Adequate

Every frontmatter token type-checks against design-md-spec.md, and every {path.to.token} reference in the components block and prose resolves (all ~30 references spot-checked). high

Load-bearing muted grays asserted AA-verified but fail AA on white

DESIGN.md frontmatter colors; EXPERIENCE.md § Accessibility Floor
label-muted #7a8aa0 on surface #ffffff computes to ~3.25:1 and na #9aa7b6 to ~2.6:1, both below AA 4.5:1 for normal text — yet these carry load-bearing content: label-muted is the info-row keys (Address/Hours/Website, 12px/700 label-caps, not large text) and na is the "Not available" / "No photo yet" text. Both § Accessibility Floor and § Foundation assert the palette is AA-verified; downstream will mirror the tokens and ship failing contrast believing it verified.
Fix: Darken label-muted to ≥ ~#5f7086 and na to ≥ ~#6b7a8c to clear 4.5:1 on white, or state the exact ratio + intended text size for each and downgrade any combo deliberately kept below AA.

Accessibility review

review-accessibility.md

The behavioral spine is unusually accessibility-literate for a draft — landmarks, heading order, aria-disabled semantics, "absence is spoken," keyboard reachability and 44px targets are all named in the EXPERIENCE Accessibility Floor. But the DESIGN palette writes checks the floor can't cash: five load-bearing text combinations fail WCAG 2.1 AA for normal text, and two of them (the amber rating value and the "Not available" gray) fail even the 3:1 large/graphic threshold. The self-congratulatory frontmatter claim "Azure palette verified for AA text on its intended surfaces" is false as measured, and the hero's legibility guarantee is asserted, not engineered. Treat contrast and the hero scrim as blockers before build.

critical

"Not available" / "No photo yet" gray is unreadable at 2.45:1

DESIGN.md Colors; EXPERIENCE Voice/Data tables
The na gray (#9aa7b6, 2.45:1) carries genuine content the spec explicitly wants read ("absence is spoken, not silent"), yet it's unreadable at any size. The intent ("visibly softer, honestly absent") is legitimate, but 2.45 makes it invisible, not soft.
Fix: Darken to ≥4.5:1 (around #6b7c8f or darker) and signal "soft" with italic + weight, not with sub-threshold contrast.
critical

Star rating amber fails both graphic (3:1) and text (4.5:1) thresholds

DESIGN nearby-card rating-color; booking panel
The amber (#f0a92c, 2.02:1) fails the 3:1 graphic threshold for the ★ glyph and the 4.5:1 text threshold for the numeric value it colors. A rating is decision-relevant data on the nearby rail, not decoration.
Fix: Use amber only for the star fill on a darker plate and render the numeric value in ink/slate; or darken the amber substantially (amber rarely clears 4.5 on white — reserve it for a large glyph paired with a dark value).
critical

Open-now badge — the one status signal — fails AA on its own tint

DESIGN open-now-badge
The green (#1f9d5b) is 3.48:1 on white and 3.11:1 on its actual rendered 10% tint (#e9f5ef) at 11px/700. The single status signal on the page fails normal-text AA.
Fix: Darken the green to ≥4.5:1 on the tint (around #137a45), or drop the tint and increase the text size; keep the dot decorative as planned.
critical

Teal taxonomy text fails AA at 12px caps

DESIGN teal usage; typography label-caps
Teal (#12a3a3, 3.09:1) is used for the category eyebrow (hero + panel) and the nearby-card kind label, both at 12px caps. Fails normal AA; the 12px size disqualifies the large-text exemption.
Fix: Darken teal to ≥4.5:1 (around #0d7d7d) for any text role, keeping the brighter teal for non-text accents only.
critical

Info-row keys in label-muted fail AA — the row's meaning depends on them

DESIGN info-row label-color
Info-row keys in label-muted (#7a8aa0, 3.52:1) at 12px/700 are the "Address / Hours / Website" labels — the row's meaning depends on them. Fails normal AA.
Fix: Darken to ≥4.5:1 (around #5c6b80).
high

Hero legibility guarantee is asserted, not engineered

DESIGN hero scrim; EXPERIENCE Accessibility Floor
The scrim is linear-gradient(scrim-soft 40% → scrim-strong): only the bottom ~60% darkens and the top 40% is near-transparent (0.05 alpha). White title (42px/800) upper glyph mass — and especially the eyebrow badge above the title — can land in the weak-scrim zone; over a bright/white photo white text there approaches 1:1. No text-shadow and no minimum floor is specified.
Fix: Specify a solid dark gradient floor reaching the full text band, add a text-shadow/halo on the title and eyebrow, and set a measurable minimum (white ≥4.5:1) at the top of the text block, not just the bottom.
high

Category eyebrow badge actively harms legibility

DESIGN hero
A rgba(255,255,255,0.16) fill with white text and a rgba(255,255,255,0.4) border: a translucent-white plate under white text adds nothing over a light photo and washes the text out.
Fix: Use a dark translucent plate (e.g. rgba(6,24,40,0.55)) behind the eyebrow so white text has a guaranteed dark backing, or ink text on a solid light chip.
high

No focus-visible indicator defined anywhere in DESIGN

DESIGN (no focus token); EXPERIENCE Accessibility Floor
EXPERIENCE promises "a visible focus ring" on Back, carousel controls, map, each nearby card, the external link, and Add-to-Trip, but no ring color, width, or offset token exists, so it can't be verified against the 3:1 non-text-contrast rule (WCAG 1.4.11) — and a default UA outline will contrast inconsistently across white/photo/blue-button backgrounds.
Fix: Define a focus token (e.g. 2px ring + 2px offset in primary or a dark ink) and verify ≥3:1 against every surface it lands on, including the blue button and the photo.
high

Disabled Add-to-Trip is the wrong pattern for the logged-out gate

DESIGN back/CTA; EXPERIENCE Add-to-Trip Flow
DESIGN says "not clickable, cursor: not-allowed" while EXPERIENCE says use aria-disabled with a why-name — a conflict. A truly disabled button is removed from the tab order and many SR users never encounter it, so the "Log in to add to your trip" rationale is only discoverable by chance. The gate is the whole logged-out conversion path.
Fix: Make it an enabled control (button or link) that routes to login with return-here, labeled "Log in to add to your trip" — keyboard-focusable, announced, actionable in one step; reserve visual "disabled" styling only.
medium

Touch targets unspecified or too small

DESIGN back-control; carousel dots
The back control is a 13px-text chip with 4px radius and no min-height; carousel dot indicators are inherently small; both fall short of 44×44px unless padded. EXPERIENCE only asserts the sticky-bar CTA and nearby cards meet 44px.
Fix: Set explicit ≥44px hit areas (padding/invisible hit slop) for the back control, each carousel dot, and desktop carousel arrows.
medium

Map keyboard operability claimed but fragile

EXPERIENCE Map behavior
Leaflet is keyboard-pannable only when the container has focus and keyboard: true, and pinch/scroll-zoom have no keyboard equivalent unless the +/− control is focusable.
Fix: Require focusable zoom buttons and a focusable map container in the spec, and confirm the OSM attribution link is in the tab order and legible at AA (Leaflet's default attribution gray often isn't).
medium

Map has no non-visual alternative for "where it is"

EXPERIENCE Map marker name; Flow 1
The marker's accessible name is just the attraction name — it conveys no location or distance to a SR user, and the page's spatial value ("gauge how far from my guesthouse") is visual-only.
Fix: Ensure the Address info row always carries the textual location, and consider an SR-only summary (e.g. "Map showing <name> at <area>").
medium

Reduced motion is unaddressed

EXPERIENCE Interaction Primitives
EXPERIENCE bans auto-advance (good) but nothing scopes prefers-reduced-motion for the nearby-card hover lift, carousel frame transitions, or sticky-panel motion.
Fix: Add a reduced-motion clause that disables transitions/lifts and any carousel slide animation.
medium

New-tab website link lacks a new-context warning

EXPERIENCE Website value; WCAG 3.2.5
rel="noopener noreferrer" is set, but nothing tells sighted or SR users the link opens a new tab.
Fix: Add a visible/aria "(opens in new tab)" affordance on the Website value.
low

Card/panel boundaries rely on a sub-3:1 hairline

DESIGN hairline #d7e1ea
The #d7e1ea hairline is 1.33:1 on white and 1.23:1 on canvas, and the white-on-canvas tonal delta is tiny, so container edges are near-invisible to low-vision users. Not a strict failure (boundaries aren't the only cue) but weak.
Fix: Darken the hairline toward ~3:1 or accept the single soft shadow on more surfaces.
low

Optional hero location line is small semi-transparent white over photo

DESIGN hero
White at 92% opacity in 500 weight compounds the hero-scrim risk above.
Fix: Full-opacity white and include it in the guaranteed-scrim band, or treat as non-essential.
low

Disabled-button text is 1.4.3-exempt but should read gated, not dead

DESIGN disabled state
Disabled-button text at 2.02:1 is technically exempt, but if the enabled-control fix is adopted the styling should still read as interactive-but-gated.
Fix: Ensure the paired "Log in" link (primary, 4.86:1) remains the focusable, high-contrast affordance.
low

Confirm heading hierarchy renders as promised

DESIGN panel-title; EXPERIENCE heading intent
Name = single <h1>, section labels = <h2> in reading order, and the booking-panel repeated name must not be a competing <h1>.
Fix: Ensure DESIGN's panel-title does not emit a second <h1> (should be a non-heading or <h2>/<p>).

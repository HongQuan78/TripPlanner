Trip Planner — Landing page directions

Three landing compositions for / (SearchPage), rendered against the Horizon token layer. Ambition level: elevate the landing page — no new hex, no new typeface, new composition. Pick one; the mechanics below the directions are already settled and identical in all three.

What varies between A, B and C: the hero's relationship to the page frame, where the eye lands first, how the destination tiles are shaped, and how the how-it-works band reads.
What does not vary: the search-overlay mechanics, the chosen-destination collapse, and the a11y contract — you already decided those, and they're shown once at the bottom.
Gradients here are the designed deliverable, not placeholders — you blessed stand-ins as shippable, so each direction carries its own layered recipe built only from Horizon tokens. Photography drops into the same slot later without touching layout.
A

Full-Bleed Immersion

The hero breaks out of AppLayout's 72rem frame and runs edge to edge, 30rem tall, with the headline stepped up to 60px and the search bar sitting large and centered near the bottom edge. It reads like a consumer travel marketplace: the page's first screen is one image and one question. Destination tiles are tall 4:5 portraits — six across, magazine-cover proportions.

Register: confident, commercial, high-impact · Eye lands: headline, then the bar · Conflict to note: overturns DESIGN.md's "framed rounded band, not a viewport bleed" layout rule
